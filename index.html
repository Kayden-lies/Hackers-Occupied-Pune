ABOUT

PUNE

Every project has a story. The problem that started it, the ideas that shaped it, the failures that changed it, and the people who kept building. But too often, we only see the final product.

Hackers Occupied Pune is here to change that.

With Pune as the launchpad for Hackers Occupied, we aim to bring together the city's growing developer and creator community around one simple idea: builders should have a voice too. AIDN and Genesis are bringing developers, creators, and problem solvers together for an experience that goes beyond the usual hackathon.

Yes, this is still a hardcore hackathon. You'll build crazy things, pull late nights, break things, fix them, and ship projects that matter. But there's another side to being a builder that deserves just as much attention.

Knowing how to build is only the beginning. Every project comes with a problem, a process, a set of decisions, failures, and breakthroughs that shape the final result. We want that journey to be part of the experience too.

So at Hackers Occupied Pune, we're celebrating two sides of the creator inside every builder:

THE VIDEO CREATORS

Those who can turn a 2 AM debugging session, a breakthrough, or a failed build into a Reel that people can relate to.

THE STORYTELLERS

Those who can put their journey into words, sharing what they're building, the technology behind it, why they chose their approach, and the impact they hope to create.

At Hackers Occupied Pune, your project is more than a submission. It's an opportunity to show how you think, what you create, and why it matters. From the first idea to the final build, we want to give the people behind the projects a platform to be seen and heard.

RULES

Know the rules. Build fair.

Official problem statements will be revealed exclusively at the venue during the opening ceremony. Teams must develop their solutions during the official hacking period.

All submissions must be the original work of the participating team. Open-source libraries, APIs, and AI-powered development tools are permitted. Plagiarism, copied work, or fraudulent submissions will result in immediate disqualification.

Each team must consist of 3–5 members.

Teams may comprise students, working professionals, or a combination of both.

Team composition cannot be changed once the hacking period begins without approval from the organizing committee.

All required deliverables, including project submissions and Creator Challenge entries (where applicable), must be submitted before the official submission deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.

Every team must present a working prototype during the judging session. Teams should be prepared to explain their solution, technical implementation, and answer questions from the judging panel.

Teams participating in the Creator Challenge must:

  • Publish content within the official evaluation window.
  • Use the official event hashtag(s).
  • Tag the designated event and sponsor accounts.
  • Submit only genuine engagement data for evaluation.

Only eligible submissions will be considered for Creator Challenge scoring.

All participants must adhere to the Hackers Occupied Code of Conduct. Treat fellow participants, mentors, judges, volunteers, organizers, sponsors, and venue staff with professionalism, respect, and inclusivity at all times.

Any attempt to interfere with another team's work, infrastructure, software, hardware, network, or event resources is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification.

Hackers Occupied follows a zero-tolerance policy towards cheating and fraudulent conduct.

The following actions are strictly prohibited:

  • Plagiarism or copying another team's work.
  • Submission of false or misleading information.
  • Manipulation or falsification of Creator Challenge metrics.
  • Use of bots, purchased engagement, fake accounts, or artificial traffic.
  • Any attempt to deceive judges or organizers.
  • Any other form of academic or competitive misconduct.

Violation of any of the above may result in one or more of the following actions:

  • Immediate disqualification of the entire team.
  • Permanent ban of all team members from participating in any current or future Genesis events.
  • Forfeiture of prizes, certificates, and any other event benefits.
  • A penalty of ₹5,000 per team, where applicable under the event's registration terms and conditions.

The decision of the organizing committee shall be final and binding.

The organizing committee reserves the right to interpret, amend, or enforce these rules whenever necessary to ensure the fair and successful conduct of the event. All decisions made by the organizers and judging panel shall be final.
